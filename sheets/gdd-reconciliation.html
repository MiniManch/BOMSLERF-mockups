04Fully specified, no ticket anywhere

These are not gaps in the spec — they are gaps in Jira. Each one is written up in detail in the repo, several with visual direction aimed straight at Amir.

  • Towers — the one I have raised three times, now beyond argument. 4 towers, 7,000 HP, 220 damage every 1.5 s, 5.5 m. Two towers standing locks that base's flag completely. Only Basic Attack damages a tower — Skill and Ultimate do nothing. "Shield of the Kingdom": 90% damage reduction while you have no minions of your own in range, applied last in the damage order, with a full visual spec (team-coloured dome, sparks, small grey damage numbers with a shield icon, 0.5 s shatter). Towers do not block projectiles. Plus an aggro-priority ladder and a defence-lock rule keyed to the attacker's position at the moment the server confirms the hit.
  • Minions — 3 per lane every 30 s, with their own targeting priority and tower interaction.
  • Teleport — replaces the old Endpoint outright. Each minion entering gives +0.2% damage and +0.2% max HP, uncapped, plus healing. The old "+0.5%, capped at +6%" is cancelled.
  • Gas — none until 11:00. The "gas at 2:15" rule is explicitly dead. In Knockdown it closes from all four walls over 90 seconds.
  • Jungle creatures — 3-minute buff, creatures respawn, and attacking them does not expose you.
  • The Captain — §11, declared at 0:00 with a swap window until 1:00. The repo lists "when the captain enters the code" as your open item (E6).
  • Saboteur — permanent passive for every Thief, +15% against towers, Basic Attack only.
And the roster. 36 characters across five categories, each with full stats, four abilities, hidden combos and art. Jira has two. The repo itself flags this as open decision C1 — 36 specified against a launch vision of 12–16 — so it is a scoping conversation with Steve, not a backlog-stuffing exercise. The four free starters are already chosen: Sir Aldric, Roland, Cassius and Mira. Note that Mother Greta is a 1,000-coin Common unlock, not a starter — which affects how BOM-41 and BOM-121 should treat her locked slot.
