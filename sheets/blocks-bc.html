Combat Mechanics Core — Status Effects, Shields & Damage Modifiers
Backend · LeonNew systemBlocks B5 B6 B8
Purpose

Today the server knows how to do exactly one thing to a player: subtract HP. Every Tank in the roster — all four, not just Greta — depends on mechanics that do not exist: stuns, knockback, slows, temporary shields, percentage damage reduction, and damage redirection between players. Without these, Greta's Skill and Ultimate cannot be built, Aldric's corrected abilities cannot be built, and Vargoth and Lorvak in a later sprint are blocked too.

This ticket builds them once, generically, as a status-effect layer the server owns. It is the single highest-value backend ticket in Sprint 4.

Blocked by

Builds on

Authority
Server-only. The client renders effects and never applies them. A client claiming "I am stunned" is ignored.

Part 1 — Status effect framework
1
Generic status effect container
foundation

A player entity carries a list of active status effects. Each effect has: type, magnitude, duration_remaining, source_player_id, source_ability_id. The server ticks them down and expires them. Effects of the same type from different sources stack by the rule defined per type below.

Effect typeBehaviourStacking rule
STUNNo movement, no abilities, no basic attack. Camera stays live.Longest duration wins — never additive
SLOWMovement speed multiplied by magnitudeStrongest slow wins
KNOCKBACKForced displacement over a set time. No player control during it.Does not stack — second one is ignored while active
SHIELDTemporary HP pool absorbed before real HPMultiple shields stack as separate pools, consumed oldest first
DMG_REDUCTIONIncoming damage multiplied by (1 − magnitude)Multiplicative, not additive — two −40% effects give −64%, not −80%
DMG_AMPLIFYOutgoing damage multiplied by (1 + magnitude)Multiplicative
CC_IMMUNITYRejects all STUN / SLOW / KNOCKBACK / PULL on applicationLongest duration wins
DMG_REDIRECTA % of damage to this player is dealt to a linked player insteadDoes not stack — one redirect per target at a time
2
Damage pipeline — strict order of operations
critical

Every point of damage in the game flows through one function, in this exact order. Getting the order wrong changes the balance of every character.

StepOperation
1Start with raw ability damage
2Apply attacker's DMG_AMPLIFY (Greta's bear form +30%)
3Apply defender's DMG_REDUCTION (Aldric's Defensive Stance −40%, Greta's aura −5%, bear form −20%)
4Check defender's CC_IMMUNITY — if the damage carried CC, the CC is dropped but damage still lands
5Apply DMG_REDIRECT — split the result, send the redirected share to the linked player as a separate damage event that re-enters this pipeline at step 3 for that player
6Subtract from SHIELD pools (oldest first) before real HP
7Subtract remainder from HP. If HP ≤ 0, fire death event.
Redirect recursion guard: redirected damage must never itself be redirected. If Greta is shielding an ally and another Greta is shielding her, damage resolves one hop only. Depth limit = 1.
3
Client event broadcast
for Block C

Every status change fires a typed event to the clients that can see that player. Block C's visual effects all hook to these. Without this, the VFX tickets have nothing to listen to.

Events: status_applied, status_expired, damage_dealt (with amount, source, whether shielded, whether redirected), shield_broken, cc_immunity_blocked.

Out of scope
PULL is listed in the type table for future use (Vargoth's Throne of Iron) but is not implemented in this ticket — Vargoth is not in Sprint 4. Crit chance and crit multiplier are not part of this system and are not being built. Per-character ability logic lives in B5, not here — this ticket only builds the mechanics those abilities call.

Continuous proximity check. Every ally within 3 m of Greta carries a −5% DMG_REDUCTION effect, refreshed while in range and removed the moment they leave.

The trap: the aura does not apply to Greta herself. The spec is explicit about this. It is the easiest thing in her kit to get wrong.

Part 2 — Skill · Guardian's Embrace

The most mechanically novel ability in the game. Greta names an ally; for 4 seconds half of everything that hits them hits her instead.

ParameterValue
Cooldown12 s
Select range8 m
Duration4 s
Redirect share50% of incoming damage
Valid targetliving ally, not self, within 8 m at cast time
Cases the server must decide, not the client:
• Target walks beyond 8 m after the cast → link persists for the full 4 s. Range is checked at cast time only.
• Target dies during the link → link ends immediately, no further redirect.
• Greta dies while a link is active → link ends immediately. The ally takes full damage from that point. Greta's death is not delayed or prevented by the link.
• Redirected damage kills Greta → she dies normally, the killer credited is whoever dealt the original damage.
• Greta transforms into a bear with a link active → link persists, and the bear's −20% reduction applies to the redirected damage.

Backend · LeonFrontend · AmirNew system
Purpose

Three of the four Tanks have hidden combos. Aldric deliberately has none. Greta is the first character to need the system, and building it generically now means Vargoth and Lorvak drop in without new plumbing later.

"Hidden" means undocumented in-game — players discover them. It does not mean invisible: when a combo fires, the player must be able to tell something good happened, or they will never learn the pattern.

Screen Shake & Camera Impact Response
VFXFrontend · Amir
Purpose

Shake is what makes a heavy character feel heavy. The Tank Spec literally calls for it — Greta's Ultimate is described as "bear growl + slight screen shake." Used sparingly it adds weight; used on every hit it induces nausea. The intensity table below is the whole ticket.

Prerequisites
Blocked by
B0 — needs damage and status events to hook to
Config
A master shake multiplier in BOM-87 so the whole system can be dialled down after playtest without touching code
Part 1 — Intensity table
EventAmplitudeDurationApplies to
Local player takes a basic hitvery light0.08 slocal only
Local player takes ability damagelight0.15 slocal only
Local player diesmedium0.4 slocal only
Greta's Ultimate AoE lands on youstrong0.5 severyone in the 4 m radius
Greta's bear transformation nearbymedium0.3 severyone within 8 m
Aldric's Hero's Sacrifice stunmedium0.3 severyone stunned by it
Knockdown Mode activatesstrong0.6 sall 4 players

Part 2 — Rules that keep it comfortable

Never on dealing damage. Shake on your own attacks at one per second is unplayable. Only on taking damage or on big events.

Never stacks. Two events in the same window use the stronger one, they do not add. Three simultaneous hits should not triple the shake.

Always decays. Every shake eases out to zero. A hard cut back to centre reads as a glitch.

Settings toggle. A "reduce screen shake" option in settings that scales everything to zero. Some players get motion sick, and this is standard accessibility practice.

Out of scope
Camera zoom effects, slow motion and the deathcam camera move (BOM-51) are separate. Controller rumble — there are no controllers.
