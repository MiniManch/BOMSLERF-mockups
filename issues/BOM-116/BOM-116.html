B4 — Bear Transformation
Frontend · the signature moment

Greta's Ultimate is the most visually dramatic thing in the game. For six seconds she stops being a woman and becomes a giant bear. This is a model swap, not a scale change, and it deserves its own ticket.

NEW · Sprint 4 · depends on B1 B2 B3
Mother Greta — Bear Form Transformation & Animation Set
Frontend · AmirVFX overlapNew pattern
Purpose

No character in BomSlerf changes model mid-match. Greta is the first, and the pattern built here will be reused by any future transformation character. The moment has to land visually or the Ultimate feels like a stat buff with a particle effect.

1
Bear form needs its own animations

The bear is a quadruped. Human animations will not retarget onto it. Minimum set required: bear idle, bear run, bear attack. Death while transformed is an edge case — see below.

2
Edge cases that will break this if unhandled
test these
CaseRequired behaviour
Greta dies while transformedBear plays a death animation and the body stays as a bear until respawn. She does not revert to human on death — reverting mid-death reads as a bug.
Greta is carrying the flag when she transformsFlag stays attached, now mounted on the bear. Flag carrier rules still apply: 15% slower, visible to everyone.
Match ends mid-transformationForm is dropped, scoreboard shows her human portrait.
Greta is stunned during the 6 secondsShe stays a bear and remains stunned. The form does not grant CC immunity — only Aldric's Ultimate does that.
Final Stage teleport fires mid-transformationForm is cancelled on teleport, cooldowns reset per the Final Stage rules.
Out of scope
The AoE damage and stun are applied by the server in B5 — this ticket renders the moment, it does not deal the damage. The golden burst particle system is specified in C4 but built here since it masks the model swap.
