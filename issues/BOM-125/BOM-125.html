Mother Greta — Ability Visual Effects
VFXFrontend · Amir
Purpose

Greta's whole identity is protecting other people, and protection is invisible by nature. Without strong visuals, playing her feels like watching other people fight. The golden tether of Guardian's Embrace is the single most important effect in Block C — it is the only thing that shows a player they are being protected.

Prerequisites
Blocked by
B5 ability logic · B6 HUD wiring · B2 model imported
Overlaps
The transformation burst is built in B4 because it masks the model swap — specified here, implemented there
Part 1 — Passive · Mother Bear's Aura

A soft brown-gold light radiates from Greta out to 3 m. Allies standing inside it carry a faint matching glow at their feet, so each protected player can see for themselves that they are in range.

The aura must be subtle — it is on permanently for the whole match. Anything bright becomes visual noise within a minute.
