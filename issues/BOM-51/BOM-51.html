Deathcam — Cut to Killer

What the player sees in the three seconds after dying. Right now the screen just freezes on the corpse and respawns — the player never learns who killed them or why, which is the single most common complaint in any arena game that ships without this.

Why cut to the killer and not a free camera. A free camera is more work and teaches the player nothing. One framed shot of who killed you, with their remaining HP, answers the only question the player has — was that close? — and takes a fraction of the effort.

Rules
Respawn delay
from config in BOM-87 — not hardcoded
Skippable
yes — a tap goes straight to the countdown
Killer disconnected
skip frame 2, show the countdown only
Killed by environment
skip frame 2 — no killer to frame
Killer dies during the cam
the shot holds; it is a snapshot, not a live feed
Teammate HP bars
stay visible throughout — the player is still reading the fight
Do not let input through. Attack and ability buttons must be dead for the whole sequence. A queued tap firing on respawn is how players lose an ability the instant they come back.
Open: respawn delay is currently unspecified anywhere. 3 s is drawn here as a placeholder. Needs a number before this is built.
