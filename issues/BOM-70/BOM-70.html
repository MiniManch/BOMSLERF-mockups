Grass Exit — Vision Reset

This ticket had the in-game HUD mockup attached to it, which specs the HP warning and ability cooldowns and says nothing about grass. It is now drawn properly. All four frames are your view; the red figure is an enemy sitting in the grass the whole time.

why it matters
It turns grass into a free scouting tool. Walk in, sweep, walk out, and you keep seeing everyone inside for another second and a half — long enough to line up an attack on someone who believes they are hidden. The ambusher's entire advantage is cancelled, which is the whole point of the mechanic.
The only exception. If he attacks, he is revealed under the normal reveal rules — leaving the grass does not protect you from seeing an attacker.
