Purpose

BOM-41 gave the home screen a character card with one selectable character — enough when there was only Aldric. With Greta there is a real choice to make, and the player needs enough information to make it: stats side by side, what each ability does, and who the character is.

CONFIRM
Four slots on screen — two playable, two locked placeholders so the roster reads as growing rather than finished. Each card carries HP, damage and the four ability icons. Tapping a card shows its full ability text; the quote line changes to that character's. Selected card gets a gold border.
Out of scope
Unlocking characters with SlerfCoin is not built — both are free and available. Character skins, the lore/backstory panel and the 3D rotating preview are later sprints. The select confirm sound is a separate audio ticket.

←

←
