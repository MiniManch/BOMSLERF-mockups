Hit Feedback — Flash, Damage Numbers & Impact Particles
VFXFrontend · Amir
Purpose

Right now the only evidence that an attack connected is an HP bar shrinking. There is no flash, no number, no particle, no sound. Players cannot tell whether they hit, missed, or were out of range. This single ticket does more for how the game feels than anything else in the sprint.

It also carries a mechanic the game needs for Block B: gold damage numbers are how a player learns a hidden combo fired.

Prerequisites
Blocked by
B0 — needs the damage_dealt event with amount, source, shielded flag and redirected flag
Pairs with
BOM-104 hit and kill sound — the sound and the flash must land on the same frame
Part 1 — Hit flash

On every confirmed hit, the target's model flashes white for 0.08 s then returns to normal over 0.12 s. Short enough not to be annoying at one hit per second, long enough to register.

Part 2 — Floating damage numbers

A number rises from the hit point, drifting up and fading over 0.8 s. Colour carries meaning:

Scope control: only show numbers for damage the local player dealt, or damage the local player took. Rendering every number in a 4v4 fight is unreadable on a phone.

Part 3 — Impact particles

A small burst at the point of contact, oriented away from the attacker. Per-character so hits read differently: Aldric throws metallic sparks from the sword, Greta leaves golden claw-slash streaks. 6–10 particles, 0.3 s lifetime, no lingering smoke.

C2 — Screen Shake & Camera Feel
Frontend · weight and impact
NEW · Sprint 4 · Block C
Screen Shake & Camera Impact Response
VFXFrontend · Amir
Purpose

Shake is what makes a heavy character feel heavy. The Tank Spec literally calls for it — Greta's Ultimate is described as "bear growl + slight screen shake." Used sparingly it adds weight; used on every hit it induces nausea. The intensity table below is the whole ticket.

Prerequisites
Blocked by
B0 — needs damage and status events to hook to
Config
A master shake multiplier in BOM-87 so the whole system can be dialled down after playtest without touching code

Part 2 — Rules that keep it comfortable

Never on dealing damage. Shake on your own attacks at one per second is unplayable. Only on taking damage or on big events.

Never stacks. Two events in the same window use the stronger one, they do not add. Three simultaneous hits should not triple the shake.

Always decays. Every shake eases out to zero. A hard cut back to centre reads as a glitch.

Settings toggle. A "reduce screen shake" option in settings that scales everything to zero. Some players get motion sick, and this is standard accessibility practice.

Out of scope
Camera zoom effects, slow motion and the deathcam camera move (BOM-51) are separate. Controller rumble — there are no controllers.
