Mother Greta — Full Animation Set
Frontend · Amir
Purpose

Greta's animations are listed verbatim in the Tank Spec and each one carries character. Her death animation in particular — she falls to the side with her hand reaching toward a nearby ally, a last act of protection — is the kind of detail that makes a character feel written rather than assembled.

Blocked by

Death and respawn must fire from server events, not client prediction

Out of scope
The bear transformation and all bear-form animations are B4 — a separate model with its own animation set. The golden visual effects that accompany these animations are Block C (C4) — this ticket is skeletal animation only.
