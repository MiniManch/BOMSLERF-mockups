Sir Aldric — Ability Visual Effects
VFXFrontend · AmirCorrected spec
Purpose

Aldric's abilities currently have no visual presence. A −40% damage reduction that looks like nothing is a stat the player has to take on faith. Each of his three abilities needs a visual that communicates its actual mechanic.

Prerequisites
Blocked by
The BOM-43 correction at the top of this document. Building VFX for "Divine Shield" wastes the work — the ability is Defensive Stance and behaves completely differently.
Needs
B0 status events — SHIELD applied, DMG_REDUCTION applied, CC_IMMUNITY applied
Part 1 — Passive · Stone Wall

The hardest to communicate and the most important, because the player controls it by not moving — which feels like doing nothing.

translucent stone-textured shell snaps around him with a brief flash · HUD passive icon lights up
at 2.0s

shell shatters into fragments and fades over 0.3s
on break

The shell's opacity tracks the remaining shield HP, so a player under fire can see their 224 draining in real time.

Part 2 — Skill · Defensive Stance

4 seconds, −40% damage, speed drops 1.8 → 1.2. The visual has to sell both halves: he is tougher and he is slower.

Shield plantShield slams forward, a faint blue hexagonal barrier flickers in front of him in the facing direction
Body auraSteady dim blue glow outlining the model for the full 4 s — signals "reduced damage" to allies and enemies alike
Slowed movementHeavy dust puffs at his feet each step, selling the weight of the reduced speed
Hits while activeDamage numbers go grey (from C1) and impact particles spark off the barrier rather than the body
ExpiryBarrier dissolves, aura fades over 0.3 s, dust stops
